BMS Simulator

4S NMC pack, 3 Ah nominal cells with manufacturing spread. C firmware running as WebAssembly.
Live in-browser sim

A simulated 4 cell lithium ion pack wired to real BMS firmware written in C, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser. Pick a scenario or drag the load and temperature sliders, inject a fault, and watch the firmware trip its protections, balance the cells and estimate state of charge.

Simulated time since the last reset or scenario start. The sim runs one firmware step per simulated second.

Pack voltage
Sum of the four cell voltages. Falls under load because of each cell's internal resistance.
–
Pack current
Current through the pack: positive is discharge, negative is charge. Zero while the contactor is open.
–
SOC (coulomb count)
Firmware state of charge from counting amp-seconds in and out. Drifts if it starts wrong; only corrected from cell voltage after 5 minutes at rest.
–
SOC (Kalman)
Firmware state of charge from a Kalman filter that compares measured cell voltage with the expected voltage. Corrects itself under load. The ± figure is its own uncertainty.
–
Cell spread
Difference between the highest and lowest cell voltage. Passive balancing bleeds the higher cells through a resistor until they are within a few mV.
–
Contactor
The main relay between the pack and the load. The firmware opens it when a protection fault trips, which cuts the current.
Protection
Firmware fault state: over and under voltage, over current, over temperature and implausible sensor readings. Faults latch until you press Clear faults and the condition is gone.
